# Design system

"Pixel-inspired, modern, premium": a warm paper background, a navy ink, one hot orange, and buttons that look like they were
cut out pixel by pixel. Everything lives in `src/app/globals.css` (tokens in `@theme`, components in `@layer components`).
In development, **`/dev/design`** shows every button family in its three states next to the palette (404 in production).

## Pixel details

* **Stair-step corners** (two steps of 2 px, `--s`) instead of rounded ones. A button is two stacked layers (`::before` = outline, `::after` = face) clipped with the same stepped polygon; the element itself is not clipped, so the focus ring and the shadow stay clean.
* **Relief**: a 1 px highlight along the top of the face and a darker ledge under it (`--ledge`, 3 px).
* **8-bit shadow** `0 2px 0 rgb(31 37 64 / .12)` as a `drop-shadow`, which follows the stepped outline.
* **States**: hover goes light (sky, or a brighter orange for the CTA, with a soft glow); pressed goes deeper and sinks 2 px; `aria-pressed` / `aria-checked` use the pressed sky; focus is a 3 px ring with a 3 px offset; disabled is 50 % opacity.

States can be forced for documentation with the helper classes `is-hover` and `is-active`.

## Accessibility

The orange CTA is a gradient from `#ea4211` to `#d63a10` with bold white text and a subtle dark text-shadow (about 4.3–4.6:1 across the face; the pure brand `#f04316` is kept for large figures and accents, where 3.8:1 is enough). Every interactive state is also readable without colour (sinking, outline, ring). Covered by the axe checks in `tests/e2e/ui.spec.ts`.

## Logo and icons

* **Logo** (`src/components/ui/Logo.tsx`): the 12 × 12 pixel globe (blue sea, orange land) and the wordmark — **"pixel" in the site's pixel font** (Jersey 10, one weight, no letter-spacing, 1.3 em so that its x-height matches the display font next to it) and **"world" in the display font** (Bricolage Grotesque 800, orange). Ink `#1f2540`, orange `#f04316`, blue `#2b35e0`.
* **Icons** are the globe alone (a wordmark is unreadable at 16 px): `src/app/icon.svg` (transparent corners, reads on light and dark tabs), `icon.png` (512), `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48) and `apple-icon.png` (180, on the site's cream because iOS paints transparency black). Next.js links them from every page by file convention. They are generated by `node scripts/build-icons.mjs` from the same grid as the logo and committed. (`tests/e2e/brand.spec.ts`.)
* The share image (`/api/og/home`) still sets its wordmark in a plain sans font: it would need the font file bundled into the serverless function.

## "100 days to save the world" — Glitch

The art direction chosen for the story (the other two mock-ups were *Arcade, the cobalt void* and *1-bit dither*). The interface stays clear and cream; only a few things "stutter". Palette added: cyan `#19D3FF`, magenta `#FF2E93`, lavender `#E9E5FF`, violet `#6A4CFF` (`--color-glitch-*`) — used as fringes and on the corrupted land, **never for running text**. Hazard stripes (orange on cream) mark the strip and the plate's header. Numbers and codes are set in the pixel font. Rules of sobriety: at most two effects at a time outside the map, no flashing faster than 3 per second, and everything still (`prefers-reduced-motion`) for those who asked. Implementation notes in `ARCHITECTURE.md → "100 days to save the world"`.
